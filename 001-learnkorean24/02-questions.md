## ① Ezoic Support — 최우선 발송

```
Subject: Grandfathered eligibility after a change of site ownership — learnkorean24.com

Hello,

I am in the process of acquiring learnkorean24.com, which is currently an Ezoic publisher
operating under a Premium/loyalty contract that runs to 22 August 2026. The site currently
receives roughly 45,000–65,000 monthly pageviews, which is below the 250,000 monthly visitor
threshold announced for new publishers in February 2026.

Before completing the purchase, I would be grateful if you could confirm the following in
writing, as each point is material to the transaction:

1. If ownership of the site transfers to me, will the site retain its grandfathered
   eligibility, or will it be assessed against the current 250,000 monthly visitor requirement?

2. Does a change of site ownership — and the associated account transfer — constitute a break
   in "continuous service" as described in your February 2026 notice?

3. What is the correct procedure to preserve eligibility: should I take over the existing
   publisher account, or create a new account and re-add the site?

4. Does the Premium/loyalty contract auto-renew on 22 August 2026? If so, what is the renewal
   fee, and may a new owner decline renewal and continue on the standard (non-Premium) tier
   without losing platform access?

5. Is there an early termination fee associated with the current contract, and would it
   transfer to a new owner?

A written confirmation would be greatly appreciated, as I cannot proceed with the purchase
without clarity on points 1 and 4.

Thank you for your help.

Kind regards,
```

## ② 매도자 — Motion Invest "Chat with Seller"

```
Subject: learnkorean24.com — due diligence questions before making an offer

Hi,

Thank you for the detailed Q&A on the listing. The site is clearly a well-built asset, and the
fact that all content was written by you and your wife — a qualified Korean teacher and a
qualified English teacher — is exactly the kind of quality I was looking for.

I am seriously interested and would like to move to an offer. Before I do, I have a set of
questions I would like to close out. Most of them concern the Ezoic arrangement, since it is
currently the only revenue source.

— EZOIC: CONTRACT AND TRANSFERABILITY —

1. Could you share a copy of the current Ezoic Premium/loyalty agreement (redacted where
   necessary)?

2. Is the Premium subscription fee currently waived under the loyalty program? And is the
   reported monthly income net of Ezoic's revenue share and of any Premium fee?

3. The contract ends on 22 August 2026. Does it auto-renew? If so, what is the renewal fee,
   and can a new owner decline renewal and continue on Ezoic's standard tier?

4. Ezoic's February 2026 notice states that grandfathered access is tied to "continuous
   service." Have you or Motion Invest confirmed with Ezoic that grandfathered eligibility
   survives a change of ownership, given the site is below the new 250,000 monthly visitor
   threshold? Any written confirmation from Ezoic would be very helpful.

— EARNINGS HISTORY —

5. Monthly income fell from roughly $295 in July 2025 to roughly $62–68 by December 2025 /
   January 2026, then recovered to roughly $137 by June 2026. Traffic appears to have been
   stable throughout, so this looks like an RPM change rather than a traffic change. What
   caused the decline, and what caused the recovery — seasonality, an Ezoic-side change,
   ad-rate softness, or something else?

— TRAFFIC AND SEO VERIFICATION —

6. Could you grant read-only access to Google Analytics and Google Search Console, or share
   12-month exports? I would particularly like to see traffic by country and by landing page,
   plus GSC clicks and impressions.

7. Has the site ever received a Google manual action, or been materially affected by a core
   update?

— YOUTUBE CHANNEL —

8. Is the channel held under a YouTube Brand Account? This determines whether primary
   ownership can be transferred cleanly.

9. Could you share a YouTube Studio screenshot of public watch hours over the last 365 days?
   I would like to understand the channel's current YPP eligibility status.

10. What is the date of the most recent upload?

— ASSETS AND HANDOVER —

11. Which registrar holds the domain, and which host serves the site? Will hosting transfer
    with the sale, or should I plan a migration? What are the annual costs?

12. Are all images, audio files and downloadable PDFs original works owned by you and fully
    transferable with the sale? Are any assets licensed from third parties?

13. Are there any email addresses associated with the domain, and will they transfer?

14. Would you be able to provide a handover and support period — for example 30 days — after
    closing?

Thank you very much. I am happy to move quickly once these points are clear.

Best regards,
```
